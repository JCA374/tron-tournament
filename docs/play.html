Arena

Pick two fighters and press Fight! Every game here was really played by the models (the two still training are refreshed every two hours), and your browser replays it move by move. Turn on territory to see the fight for space: each cell is tinted by the bike that can reach it first. The one walled into the smaller area almost always loses.

How to read it

Glowing trail = a bike's wall. White square = its head. Bikes move one cell per tick and can only turn left, go straight or turn right. A crash bursts into sparks.

Territory (the tint): cells the bike reaches first if both raced there now. The bars on the right show each bike's share. When the two areas stop touching, they're walled off from each other: from then on it's only about who fills their own area more efficiently, and the bigger area wins about 94% of the time.

Series: every pair played several games from different start positions, each position from both sides. The score at the top right is the whole series.
