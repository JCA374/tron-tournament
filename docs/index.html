The Tron duel: PPO vs Mini-AlphaZero

Five different kinds of machine learning taught themselves Tron light cycles on one 8-core laptop. After CMA-ES was knocked out on Thursday at noon, and NEAT and DQN were retired at 22:00, it's a duel: PPO (learns from rewards, now with a bonus for claiming space) against Mini-AlphaZero (thinks ahead with a tree search, now 128 simulations per move). Every 30 minutes they play each other and four fixed bots. The final is Friday at 16:00. Watch them fight in the Arena →

Leaderboard

Rating on the Elo scale, fitted to the latest ranking round only (each model's newest checkpoint). A fixed bot is pinned so ratings from different rounds can be compared: the space-filler bot at 1500 until round 7. By then the top four models beat it 20 out of 20, so the scale drifted upwards. From round 8 the stronger territory bot is pinned instead, at the rating it got in its first round. "Avg 3" is the average of the last three rounds: knockouts use that, so one unlucky round can't decide them.
