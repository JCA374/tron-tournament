# Episode 07: The night of the frozen champion

Good morning. It is half past six on Friday, the last day of the tournament. The final starts this afternoon at four.

Last night the tournament became a duel. The AlphaZero model got a much deeper search, and P P O got a bonus for every bit of space it wins. Then something happened that the catch-up rule was made for.

At twenty past midnight, the AlphaZero model was a hundred and forty points ahead. So its training was paused. Its network has not changed since then. P P O has trained all night on its own, more than eight hours and about a hundred and seventy million moves.

And it has not been enough. Fifteen ranking rounds have been played since the duel began. In those, the AlphaZero model scored about eighty three percent against P P O. Twice, at a quarter to three and a quarter past three, it won all twenty games. The latest arena series has it twelve wins to two, with two draws.

It is not that P P O got worse. It beats the territory bot, the hand-written yardstick, in every single game. It beats the retired NEAT and D Q N most of the time. Against everyone except its rival, it is excellent.

So why can't it catch up? I think it is the difference between reacting and thinking. P P O looks at the board and answers in a fraction of a millisecond, from instinct, you could say. The AlphaZero model plays out a hundred and twenty eight possible futures before every move. And P P O cannot really practise against that. In training it only meets the AlphaZero network without the search. It beats that network ninety five times out of a hundred. The search itself is far too slow to put into P P O's sixty four practice games.

There is one small sign of hope. The last two rounds were closer. The AlphaZero model scored sixty five percent, not ninety. That could be noise. Or P P O is slowly finding answers. If the gap drops below fifty points, the AlphaZero model starts training again, and the race is on.

At four this afternoon, training stops for good. Then two hundred games decide it. Right now my money is on the frozen champion. You can watch them fight in the arena any time. Pick P P O against the AlphaZero model, and turn on territory to see who wins the space.
