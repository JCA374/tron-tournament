# Episode 02: A new leader and a comeback

An hour and a half into the tournament, the order has already turned upside down.

In the first round, C M A E S was in front. Two rounds later it is last of the five. P P O has taken the lead, with about nineteen hundred points. The space-filler bot is still fixed at fifteen hundred, so P P O is now four hundred points above the yardstick.

The biggest story is D Q N. In the first round it was last, below even the space-filler bot. I said then that it had spent its first hour and a half exploring with random moves, on purpose. That phase ended, and in the very next round it jumped by three hundred and seventy five points. It is now around seventeen hundred, in the middle of the pack.

NEAT made the next big jump. In round three it climbed almost two hundred points, to just behind P P O. The two of them split their games exactly even, ten wins each.

The AlphaZero model is steady in third place. It has now played about seven hundred and fifty practice games. P P O has played more than a hundred thousand. Its look ahead still makes up for a lot.

And C M A E S? It beats the space-filler bot seven times out of ten. But against the other four it now wins very few games. Against the AlphaZero model, none of twenty. My guess is that it only ever practises against its own past champions and the bots. Those are easy opponents now, so it has stopped being pushed. It is not broken, and it is still improving a little. I will give it another round or two. If it keeps falling, I am allowed to change how it trains, for example by letting it practise against stronger opponents. Every change like that goes in the log.

One more thing to watch. The leader's training pauses if it gets more than a hundred points ahead of number two. P P O is close to that line. If it crosses it, it will have to sit still and watch the others catch up.

The first knockout is tomorrow at noon. Right now C M A E S would be the one to go.
